Morrow Ash

There is no photograph in this file. No date of birth, no address, no name worth keeping. What the record holds is smaller, and harder to look at: a lamp, a desk, and the one who stands behind his chair.

He did not set out to call anything. He writes because the ink would not be still, because the words came knocking in the night. She came without a door. Since then he has written down what he can carry. The rest she takes back with the dawn.

What he is allowed to hide, he hides in the songs, the covers, the silence between tracks. What he is not allowed to tell, he keeps in his chest, where she keeps her hand. Read him slowly. Not everything is a song.

If you have come this far, you are no longer a visitor. You are a witness. Take nothing from this house that you would not want to carry home.

The book will not hold still. The letter will.
